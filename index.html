What this demo leaves out

The feet do not travel across the floor, so direction is shown by the arrow in the text. Hip and body movement is reduced to the weight bar and a pop on the taps. There is no partner, no turns and no live feedback yet. The footwork is the standard basic from general knowledge, so have a dance teacher check the taps before lessons are built on it. With the built-in sound it is a synthesized click and shaker, not a real song. YouTube plays through its official embedded player, so the page never touches the video's audio. Tempo comes from your microphone or from tapping. Spotify links still cannot play here.
